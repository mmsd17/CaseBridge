## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional digital governance platform tailored for apex educational regulatory bodies, statutory ombudsmen, and high-throughput administrative triage. The aesthetic bridges the sovereign credibility of government regulatory oversight with the surgical clarity, precision, and efficiency of enterprise SaaS intelligence consoles.

### Core Tenets
- **Institutional Authority:** Grounded in deep imperial navy surfaces, crisp structural divisions, and formal administrative typography that projects statutory permanence and regulatory integrity.
- **Operational Vigilance:** Built for real-time triage where statutory Service Level Agreements (SLAs) are paramount. Alert states, escalation rings, and turnaround warnings take precedence without introducing visual chaos or alarmist noise.
- **High-Density Utility:** Prioritizes dense, structured data comprehension over decorative whitespace. Information is laid out for downward scan speed, rapid comparison, and unambiguous classification.
- **Restrained Modernism:** A hybrid aesthetic combining Modern Corporate SaaS refinement with surgical hairline borders, high-contrast typography, and minimal micro-elevation. Avoids glassy distractions, gratuitous gradients, and heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around functional utility, rigorous legibility, and unmistakable statutory status signaling.

### Structural & Core Tones
- **Primary Action (`#245BDB` / `#1E4CB8`):** The primary interactive driver. Used for core actions, active navigation states, interactive toggles, and primary execution controls.
- **Institutional Navy (`#102846` / `#0B1C33`):** Anchors persistent peripheral navigation, official regulatory seals, and administrative context chrome. Establishes sovereignty and structural hierarchy.
- **Apex Gold / Amber Accent (`#F0A93B`):** Dedicated to official crest insignia, badge embellishments, notification indicators in dark contexts, and statutory executive highlights.
- **Administrative Canvas (`#F6F8FC` / `#F1F4F9`):** A soft, cool administrative background engineered to eliminate glare during prolonged audit and grievance triage sessions.
- **Card & Data Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white backing for data tables, analytical charts, drawers, and operational dialogs.
- **Structural Borders (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`):** Crisp 1px hairline delimiters that separate cards, table rows, and input surfaces without visual bulk.

### Functional Status System (SLA & Lifecycle)
Status colors use a consistent tripartite token model: solid text/icon token, low-contrast background tint, and a defining 1px perimeter ring.
- **Critical & Overdue Breach:** Text/Icon `#DC2626` | Background `#FEF2F2` | Ring `#FECACA`. Applied to statutory SLA breaches, high-severity escalations, and regulatory blocks.
- **Warning & In-Progress SLA:** Text/Icon `#D97706` | Background `#FFFBEB` | Ring `#FDE68A`. Applied to pending investigations nearing SLA boundaries.
- **Compliant & Resolved:** Text/Icon `#059669` | Background `#ECFDF5` | Ring `#A7F3D0`. Applied to resolved complaints, verified certifications, and approved audits.
- **Secretariat & Bureau Routing:** Text/Icon `#4F46E5` | Background `#EEF2FF` | Ring `#C7D2FE`. Indicates ombudsman reviews, committee referrals, and inter-bureau transfers.
- **Neutral Administrative:** Text/Icon `#475569` | Background `#F1F5F9` | Ring `#E2E8F0`. Used for closed archives, draft filings, and routine metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid information ingestion, numerical alignment, and institutional clarity under dense operational conditions.

### Rules & Formatting
- **Tabular Figures:** All KPI stats, data grids, timestamps, turnaround countdowns, and case volumes must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. This prevents layout flutter during real-time data streaming or status filtering.
- **Reference Identification:** Monospaced typography (`JetBrains Mono`) is strictly enforced for tracking identifiers (e.g., `AIT/2026/0014`, institute approval IDs, and statutory circular references) to ensure error-free optical character recognition.
- **Label Discipline:** Field labels and table header titles must use `label-caps` in uppercase styling with a subtle tracking of `+0.05em` and slate secondary coloring (`#64748B`), separated by 4px from associated field data.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy implements a structured, fluid grid configured for dense data delivery, standardizing on an authoritative 4px/8px baseline rhythm.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Persistent Shell:** Desktop viewports leverage a fixed 256px (`w-64`) Imperial Navy navigation sidebar on the left margin paired with a fluid administrative canvas.
- **Content Boundary:** Main administrative views conform to a centered max width of 1280px (`max-w-[1280px]`), preventing unreadable text expansion on ultrawide monitors while keeping data within direct line of sight.
- **Metric Grids:** Standard administrative dashboards utilize a 4-column metric arrangement: `grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-4` with 16px gaps.
- **Operational Splits:** Analytical registers utilize an asymmetric 12-column layout: 7 columns dedicated to the primary complaint log or analytics charts, paired with 5 columns for SLA breakdowns, bureau distribution lists, or fast audit queues.

### Viewport Adaptations
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column stack, collapsible off-canvas navigation drawer, condensed row presentation, horizontal scroll for tables.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 2-column KPI tiles, persistent slim nav bar or responsive hamburger toggle, table views prioritizing ID, Title, Priority, and Status.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Persistent multi-tier navigation sidebar, comprehensive data tables with persistent multi-column sorting, and side-by-side analytical drawers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed using a combination of **low-contrast outlines** and **subtle micro-elevation layers**. Heavy drop shadows and ornamental glass blurs are avoided to maintain an official, high-trust administrative atmosphere.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** The soft administrative ground `#F6F8FC`. Flat, zero shadow.
- **Card & Container Surfaces (Level 1):** Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) containers defined primarily by a crisp 1px `#E2E8F0` border and an ultra-subtle micro-shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Raised Interactive Dropdowns & Popovers (Level 2):** Menus, multi-select bureau filters, and calendar selectors float on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and a focused shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Audit Detail Drawers & Modals (Level 3):** Right-side sliding complaint drawers and critical escalation modals leverage a solid `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a deep, soft elevation: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`. Backdrops use a neutral administrative scrim: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with a 1px micro-blur.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, balanced corner radius scale (`roundedness: 2`) that reinforces enterprise structure and governmental credibility.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radius (0.125rem - 0.25rem):** SLA tags, monospaced code badges, and inline status indicator dots.
- **Component Radius (0.5rem):** Form inputs, action buttons, search bars, and dropdown menus. Provides a modern feel while remaining crisp and institutional.
- **Container Radius (0.75rem - 1rem):** Dashboard cards, KPI tiles, data table containers, and flyout drawer panels.
- **Pill (Full):** Reserved exclusively for lifecycle status badges, severity pills, and notification counter bubbles to instantly distinguish status metadata from interactive rectangular controls.

- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E2E8F0` (hover `#CBD5E1`), text `#334155` (hover `#0F172A`), background hover `#F8FAFC`.
- **Critical / Reject:** Background `#FEF2F2`, border 1px `#FECACA`, text `#DC2626` (hover background `#FEE2E2`).
- **Icon Utility:** 36x36px square with 8px radius, text `#64748B`, hover background `#F1F5F9` and text `#0F172A`.

### Status & Priority Badges (Pills)
- Rendered as full pills (`rounded-full`) with uppercase tracking or medium casing, padding px-2.5 py-0.5, text size 11px.
- Built using the 3-layer token convention: background tint + contrasting text + 1px matching ring:
  - *Critical / SLA Overdue:* `#FEF2F2` bg, `#DC2626` text, `ring-1 ring-[#FECACA]`.
  - *Approaching SLA / Active:* `#FFFBEB` bg, `#D97706` text, `ring-1 ring-[#FDE68A]`.
  - *Resolved / Approved:* `#ECFDF5` bg, `#059669` text, `ring-1 ring-[#A7F3D0]`.
  - *Under Review / Secretariat:* `#EEF2FF` bg, `#4F46E5` text, `ring-1 ring-[#C7D2FE]`.

### Data Tables (Complaints Register)
- **Container:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` outer border, 12px corner radius.
- **Header Row:** Background `#F8FAFC`, border-bottom 1px `#E2E8F0`, typography in `label-caps` (`#64748B`), vertical height 40px.
- **Data Rows:** Height 48px to 54px for high vertical density. Alternating row transitions with subtle hover state `#EFF6FF/50`.
- **Separators:** 1px hairline horizontal divider `#F1F5F9`. No vertical interior cell borders.
- **Reference Cells:** Complaint IDs displayed in `code-badge` with an overdue warning badge anchored immediately adjacent if SLA is breached.

### Inputs & Administrative Filters
- **Input Fields:** Pure white background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, 8px radius, padding px-3.5 py-2, text 14px. Focus state: border `#3B82F6` accompanied by a 2px `#DBEAFE` halo ring.
- **Filter Toolbar:** Sticky horizontal cluster pairing a prominent search input with Bureau select dropdowns, SLA filter pills, and active filter reset actions.

### Cards & KPI Tiles
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` with 1px `#E2E8F0` border and 12px radius.
- **KPI StatCard:** Features a 40x40px icon receptacle with a tinted functional background (e.g. blue-50, red-50), a bold 24px tabular number (`kpi-stat`), a clear 12px slate label, and an SLA delta metric tag.
